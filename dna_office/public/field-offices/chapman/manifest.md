## Write Up

The D.N.A. opened a field office in Chapman University's Guggenheim Gallery during the exhibit *In Love With
Night*, curated by Ciara Ennis and Max King Cap, August 31 – October 2, 2009.

On September 15, 2009, the D.N.A. led a Nocturnal Walking Tour of the Chapman University campus, along with
Orange County naturalist Joel Robinson.
