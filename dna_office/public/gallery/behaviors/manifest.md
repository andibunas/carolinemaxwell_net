### 24-hour turkey farm
Medium: digital inkjet print, 2009
Image: turkeys.jpg

20,000 turkeys on a Minnesota farm. Bright floodlights fill the space at night. This artificial daylight keeps
the turkeys eating 24 hours a day. These turkeys gain weight at a much faster pace than normal, and are ready
to send to slaughter in less than a year.
