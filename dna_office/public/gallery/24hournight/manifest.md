## Synopsis

While the sun is setting in Los Angeles, the sun is rising in Tel Aviv. As night is ending in Los Angeles, night is
beginning in Tel Aviv.

## Write Up

Artworks created for the Department of Nocturnal Affairs by Tal Yizrael and Caroline Maxwell.

While the sun is setting in Los Angeles, the sun is rising in Tel Aviv. As night is ending in Los Angeles, night is
beginning in Tel Aviv. Tal Yizrael in Tel Aviv, and Caroline Maxwell in Los Angeles, photographed their cities at
night during a single 24-hour period.
