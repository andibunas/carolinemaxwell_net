### Tea Kettle Junction, Death Valley National Park, California
Image: teakettleMap.jpg

"Tea Kettle Junction," Death Valley National Park, California.

At 6 p.m. it was still 104 degrees Farenheit. I had been driving for the past 4 hours and hadn't seen a single human being. All I had to connect me to Australia was a bag of eucalyptus-dyed wool. I stopped at these crossroads to take some photos in the last remaining minutes of sunlight.
