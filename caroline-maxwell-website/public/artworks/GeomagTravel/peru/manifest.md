## Artworks

### The Racetrack, Death Valley National Park, California
Image: PERUracetrack.jpg

An ancient lake bed is dotted with stones that mysteriously "move" leaving tracks in the earth. The Nazca earth drawings in Peru are similarly continuous pathways carved into the land. A large outcropping of rock sits at the north end of The Racetrack. Machupicchu was built across from a large rocky peack called Pachamama. Small scale models of this mountain can be found at Machupicchu. Perhaps the Incas understood the usefulness of the miniature and the mirror.
