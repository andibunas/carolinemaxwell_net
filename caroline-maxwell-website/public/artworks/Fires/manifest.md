## Synopsis

Works created in response to the 2025 Eaton Wildfire

## Write Up

After The Eaton Fire:  Works created in response to the 2025 Los Angeles wildfires. The Eaton wildfire hit very close to home, as I've lived in and around this community for my entire life.  The experience of seeing our neighborhoods, mountains, and a lifetime of memories tied to a place, go up in flames overnight was one I will never forget.  Finding ways to work with this subject has been a challenge.  Some images and memories are too difficult to face. Yet, I keep returning to images and memories of people gathering and helping, showing kindness, solidarity, and overwhelming generosity.
