## Synopsis

Early paintings and installations from 2004 to 2006, including the Twins and Small Series groups.

## Write Up

A selection of work from 2004 through 2006, spanning oil and charcoal paintings on canvas alongside more experimental pieces made with tar, salt water, and dandelion seeds.
