## Synopsis

Pinhole camera photographs of endangered Engelmann Oaks, drawn over in ink made from California Black Walnuts.

## Write Up

Engelmann Oaks and the California Black Walnut are two of the most endangered native trees in California. For this series, I took pinhole camera photos of Engelmann Oaks, and with ink created from California Black Walnuts, I created drawings on top of the pinhole camera prints.
