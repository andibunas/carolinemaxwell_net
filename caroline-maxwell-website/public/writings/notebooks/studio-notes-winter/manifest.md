## Write Up

Notes kept during the coldest weeks of the January Jones series.

## Writing

The radiator in the studio makes a sound like a held breath being let go, on and off, all day. I have started timing my brushstrokes to it without meaning to.

Some days the light comes in flat and grey and every color on the palette looks like a compromise. Other days, for maybe twenty minutes around four in the afternoon, the whole room goes the color of a bruise healing, and I try to work fast enough to catch it before it changes. I rarely do. That failure is most of what this series is about.
