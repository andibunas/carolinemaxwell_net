Name: On Slowness

## Synopsis

A short entry on why the January Jones paintings took a full year to finish.

## Write Up

The paintings in this room took longer than any I have made. Not because the surfaces are complicated — most of them are just a table, a window, a fall of light — but because I kept waiting for the room to tell me it was finished, and it never quite did.

I have started to think of slowness as a material, the way I think about oil or graphite. It has a texture. It resists you in a specific way. You can push against it, and sometimes it gives, and sometimes it pushes back harder, and either way you learn something about the painting you would not have learned by finishing it quickly.

I don't think fast work is worse. I think I am just not very good at it, and I have decided to stop apologizing for that.
