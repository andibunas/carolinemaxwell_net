**title 1**\
I bought the letters for four dollars, in a shoebox that also held a broken thermometer and a lot of loose buttons. They were addressed to no one in particular — drafts, it seems, rather than letters that were ever sent — and they were all about painting, in the specific, granular way that people only write when they don't expect to be read.

## part 2
I don't know the writer's name. I know she worked in a cold room, that she distrusted her own blues, and that she once spent three weeks on a single square foot of a canvas she eventually painted over entirely. I think about her more than I think about painters I actually know, possibly because there is nothing left to argue with. She only gets to be right.
